# October app identity

`quran-app-512.png` is an unchanged copy of the Android app's
`release/new-logo/1 UPLOAD - Google Play/UPLOAD to Google Play - 512x512.png`.
It is the complete Play Store artwork, including Quran lettering. The website's
crescent-only umbrella identity remains separate.

`quran-logo-formation.mp4` reuses the owner's existing
`UmmahtiAds/Videos/ummahti-short-v3-new-logo-1440x2560.mp4`, 59.15–68.10 seconds.
The whole formation is retained: woven crescent, gathering threads, glass edge,
lettering and the resolved Play icon. The subsequent promotional end card is
outside the extract. No art, text or animation has been redrawn.

The portrait frame is cropped to a 1440 px square at x=0, y=350, then scaled to
800 px with Lanczos. All stages remain within the crop. H.264, CRF 21, 60 fps,
yuv420p, faststart; no audio track. The file is approximately 607 KB.

`quran-logo-formed.webp` is the matching crop of source frame 67.95 s, quality 92.
It is the static fallback for no JavaScript, reduced motion, data saving and
failed playback. No image treatment is applied.

Playback loads on view, runs silently once and holds its finished frame. The
visitor can pause, continue or replay. Leaving view or hiding the tab pauses
playback. Reduced motion and data saving disable automatic playback.
