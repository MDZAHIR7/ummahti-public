Fonts used by ummahtiofficial.com
=================================

cinzel-latin.woff2       Cinzel (variable weight axis), subset to latin
jakarta-latin.woff2      Plus Jakarta Sans (variable weight axis), subset to latin

Both are the same binaries the Ummahti Android app draws with, taken from
app/src/main/res/font in the app repository and subset to the latin range for
the web. No glyph outlines were altered.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full
licence text for each accompanies it in this directory, as the licence
requires. The fonts are not sold, on their own or otherwise, and the reserved
font names are unchanged.
