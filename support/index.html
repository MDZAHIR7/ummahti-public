Frequently asked questions

Does Ummahti work offline?

Yes. The complete Arabic Quran, bundled translation, search, bookmarks and core navigation are available offline. Streaming recitation and some optional resources need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. Ummahti has no account or sign-in requirement.

Where is my reading data stored?

Your reading position, bookmarks, settings and personal reading data are stored locally on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a problem?

Email ummahtichannel@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, Ummahti version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not include private reflections, bookmarks or reading history.

Where can I follow launch updates?

Visit the official Ummahti YouTube channel or join the WhatsApp community above.
