Frequently asked questions

Does Ummahti work offline?

Yes. The complete Arabic Qur’an, bundled translation, search, bookmarks, core navigation and دليل أُمّتي are available offline. Streaming recitation and some optional resources need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. Ummahti has no account or sign-in requirement.

Where is my reading data stored?

Your reading position, bookmarks, settings and personal reading data are stored locally on your device. See the سياسة الخصوصية for details.

How do I report a problem?

Email ummahtichannel@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, Ummahti version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not include private reflections, bookmarks or reading history.

What is دليل أُمّتي, and is it an AI?

No, and it is not a fatwa service either. الدليل matches what you describe against a catalogue of topics written and reviewed in advance, and answers with the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah passages held against that topic. Every source is named with its exact reference so you can open and read it yourself. The matching runs on your phone, it works with no connection, and nothing you type leaves the device.

What does الدليل do if it cannot answer me?

It never dead-ends and it never guesses. A clear match opens the topic. Several plausible matches are offered for you to pick from. A weak match offers related topics and says plainly that they are related rather than exact. A query that matches nothing is answered with the broad subjects instead of an apology.

How many reading themes are there?

Twelve: four core reading themes and eight drawn from a tradition or a place, six light and six dark. Reading المظاهر and Heritage, in Settings, shows each one with a live preview and a note on where its pattern came from. Nothing is traced or photographed; every motif is geometry drawn in code.

Does changing my theme affect anything else?

No. A theme changes appearance and nothing more. Your reading position, آية عند فتح القفل progress, playback, search and settings are untouched, your choice survives a restart and a reboot, and a theme chosen in an older version still resolves to the same theme after updating. المظاهر work fully offline and download nothing. The home-screen widget follows your choice too.

Which theme is easiest to read?

الحبر الكلاسيكي, if you want maximum legibility or a printed-Mushaf feel. It is black ink on white paper with no colour and no ambient wash. It is also the one theme that does not shift its accent on Fridays or in Ramadan, which is deliberate: a grayscale theme that is grayscale six days a week would not be worth offering.

Where can I see what each release added?

On the الجديد page, and in the app under Settings, where it reads offline.

Where can I follow launch updates?

Visit the official Ummahti قناة يوتيوب or join the WhatsApp community above.
