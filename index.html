From a focused Mushaf to guided Hifz, Ummahti brings careful reading tools together while keeping the core Quran experience available without a connection.

01 / OFFLINE

Read anywhere

The complete Arabic Quran, bundled translation, search, bookmarks and navigation work offline from the moment you install.

02 / READING

Read your way

Move between Mushaf pages, continuous reading, verse stream, Focus and Hifz modes—with four carefully set Arabic scripts.

03 / HIFZ

Remember with intention

Use guided repetition, recall and revision tools designed to support memorisation without turning worship into a score.

04 / RECITATION

Listen with continuity

Stream verse-by-verse recitation when connected, while your reading position stays exactly where you left it.

05 / PRIVATE

Private by default

No sign-in, no advertising identifier and no reading-history profile. Optional diagnostics begin disabled until you choose otherwise.

06 / PERSONAL

A reader that settles in

Choose typography, themes and reading tools while keeping bookmarks, notes and progress under your control.
