Drawn with the mushaf

The Quran,
in one crescent.

Our moon is not an illustration. Its lines are drawn with all 6,236 ayat, Al-Fatiha at the upper tip and An-Nas at the lower, so any ayah can be found where it sits, and read.

Counted the Kufan way, as numbered in the standard Hafs mushaf, from the same Quran text the app carries.

Point at the moon to light a surah. Tap any ayah to read it.

01 / 30 juz

One moon,
thirty parts.

The crescent opens along its own lines into the thirty juz. Each part is a stretch of the moon, read in order. Some hold 110 ayat and some 564, because ayat differ in length while each juz fills about twenty pages.

02 / 30 nights

A month of
moonlit nights.

Give each night of the Hijri month one juz and the Quran is complete as the month ends, as many do in Ramadan. Each moon is that night’s portion, lit as the moon would be that night.

Ramadan begins and ends with the sighted crescent, and is completed as thirty days when the crescent is hidden (Sahih Muslim 1081b). The phases shown are illustrative.

03 / My Khatm

Plan it with
My Khatm.

Choose a pace and watch the moons regroup into your daily portions. In the app, My Khatm keeps this plan, brings you back to today’s portion from Home, and adjusts when life changes.

        A few useful answers

        Before you begin.

        What is Ummahti, and what is Ummahti Quran? ＋

        Ummahti is the umbrella platform. Ummahti Quran is our free Android application. Our YouTube and Instagram channels share recitations, learning and reminders around that same purpose.

        Is the app available for my phone? ＋

        Ummahti Quran is available for Android through Google Play. We do not currently publish an iPhone app. The website works across phones, tablets and computers.

        Is Guide an AI chatbot? ＋

        Guide searches a curated, reviewed catalogue of topics and their sources. It is not unrestricted AI, a scholar or a service for personal religious rulings.

        Do I have to use my microphone? ＋

        No. Voice assistance in My Hifz is optional. You can type a reference or choose a passage manually. The app explains setup and availability, and asks for microphone permission only when you choose a voice step.

        Searching for “Ummati”? ＋

        Our name is Ummahti, with an H: U–M–M–A–H–T–I. Our official home is ummahtiofficial.com, with @Ummahti on YouTube, @ummahti_quran on Instagram and the Android app com.ummahti.quran.
